# DiaVeritas Clinical Benchmark Questions Sheet

> **Benchmark testing sheet containing verified clinical research questions tailored for DiaVeritas evidence synthesis and contradiction resolution.**

## 💡 Why These Questions Work (Methodological Rationale)

Every question in this benchmark is engineered using the clinical **PICO** formulation:
1. **Population:** Specifically targets Type 2 Diabetes or Prediabetes.
2. **Intervention / Exposure:** Uses precise, recognized medical terms (*SGLT2 inhibitors, Metformin, Aerobic exercise, Coffee, Semaglutide*) rather than ambiguous colloquial phrases.
3. **Outcome Endpoint:** Evaluates concrete laboratory or clinical endpoints (*HbA1c, MACE, heart failure hospitalizations, T2D incidence, hypoglycemia*).
4. **Directional Causative Verb:** Uses clean, active causative verbs (*reduces, improves, increases, causes, lowers*) that translate into robust NLI premise-hypothesis assertions.
5. **Corpus Grounding:** Every question is directly grounded in primary clinical trials (DPP, DARE, UKPDS, PREDIMED, EMPA-REG, DiRECT) indexed in your 15,513-chunk database.
